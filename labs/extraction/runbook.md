# Runbook RB-0417: relay restart

Written for the night shift of Outpost-K41. Read the safety note before step 4.

## Preparation

### Before you touch the relay

Confirm that reservation RES-20931 is closed
and that the quartermaster has signed the ledger.

## Steps

1. Switch the relay to standby and wait 45 seconds.
2. Pull the filter cartridge from bay-17.
3. Count the spare cartridges in the cold store.

Safety: never pull a cartridge while the relay is live; the bay stays locked until step 3 is logged.

4. Restart the relay from the console.
5. Log the restart in the ledger as RB-0417.
